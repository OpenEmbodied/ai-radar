## Signals

- **Correct before failing, and audit before trusting.**
  - PHIL lets operators preview a policy's predicted motion in XR and step in before a mistake. Rejected predictions paired with the corrections then drive preference post-training: with five corrections, X-VLA rises from 57.8% to 76.9% against HG-DAgger (first-party).
  - Trusting Imagined Futures accepts world-model "imagined" supervision for VLA post-training only when an auditor trained on real executions judges the predicted reward trustworthy.
  - Both echo this week's finding that unchecked verifiers mislead self-improving agents (2026-11-07).
- **Evaluation that separates where generalization breaks.** MESA (CoRL 2026) isolates spatial, object-instance, object-category and subtask-composition generalization, and adds a bimanual suite. The best of seven baselines reaches 52% (first-party). A 1,700-trial real dexterity study finds only 5 of 67 offline metrics track real success.

- **Embodied RAG++** (memory): capability-grounded robot-object assignment from scene graphs and manuals. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07517)
