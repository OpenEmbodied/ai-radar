# Radar · 2026-11-25

## Signals

- **What makes a good embodied harness, now measured.** Tuesday's listing (191 cs.RO entries) is the largest of the month, and it reads like a field taking stock of its own tooling. All figures below are first-party.
  - **Embo-Harness** tests prompting, planning, action execution and tool use one at a time, and distills six findings into a recipe: rely on the model's native visual reasoning and tool use rather than handcrafted plans, skills or action priors. The recipe reaches 83.3% on RoboLab-120, 26.2 points above the best reported baseline, and succeeds in 15 of 20 zero-shot real trials. Accurate spatial target estimation matters more than generating joints or trajectories.
  - **RUIBench** crosses six action abstractions with two interaction forms into 13 robot-use interfaces. Changing only the interface moves a frontier agent from 45% to 81% success, and a fine-tuned 4B model from 13% to 47%. It also finds a verification gap: agents often declare success despite failures they could see.
  - **RoboUniverse-10K** releases 10,807 verifiable physics environments built from real videos, each inside a harness that hides the grading logic, so robot agents can be post-trained the way coding agents are. Its 35B-A3B model lifts zero-shot success from 0.8% to 21.2% on RoboUniverse-Verified.
- **Safety and memory fail differently under closed loop.**
  - **SayDo** runs the same hazard scenarios along a scale from chat to closed-loop control of a simulated arm. Hazardous-action rates rise from 14% in chat to 47% in the simulator, and up to 80% for the most affected model. Text-only safety evaluations do not predict how a model behaves as a robot controller.
  - **RoboLiveMem** lets the world keep moving while the model thinks. Claude and GPT models drop from 97% success with the world paused to 50% with it running, mostly because they act on camera frames from before the change finished.
  - **WorldLeague** finds that VLMs which answer every final question correctly still make zero physical progress in 71.8% of those episodes.
- **Agent and policy, divided better.**
  - **RoboHarness-Zero** needs no per-task preparation: it lifts a frozen VLA from 57.5% to 70.5% while keeping 90.4% of the VLA's standalone successes.
  - **Agent on Guard** learns when a programming agent should step in, cutting tokens by 85.1%.
  - **RoboThink** trains the policy to follow a watching agent's corrections mid-task.
  - **ReWeave** co-optimizes the harness and a task topology, taking RoboDojo from 25.50% to 58.25%.
  - **Contact Harness** lets GPT-6 Astra read force feedback and insert 15 connector types 90.7% of the time.
  - **BAAI's Innata-0** designs a world-action model and an agent together.
