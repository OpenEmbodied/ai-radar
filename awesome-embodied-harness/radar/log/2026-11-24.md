# Radar · 2026-11-24

## Signals

- **Recursive harness self-improvement becomes a recipe.** Three papers improve a frozen model by revising its harness from execution evidence, with no weight updates (all first-party):
  - **Embodied-Harness**, a paper that shares this list's name, pairs an Actor that controls the robot with a Reflector that turns evidence into reusable guidance. It reports 82.4% on RoboTwin 2.0, 76.7% on RoboSPA and 94.4% on RMBench.
  - **NavRSI** has a designer agent rewrite a navigation harness using simulator diagnostics that the navigator itself never sees, reaching 85.64% on R2R-CE val-unseen with GPT-6-Astra from a single RGB camera.
  - **EvoTER** keeps a revision to its tool-use skills only if it raises complete-task success in fresh sessions, lifting simulation success from 53.60% to 80.40%.

  They share one pattern: separate acting from diagnosing, keep only changes that are validated, and carry experience across rounds. This continues the self-improvement line of 2026-11-11 (ReSkill) and 2026-11-17 (ESI).
- **Agents that program their own interrupts.** A turn-based harness is blind while a VLA call runs. TRIP has the agent write a tripwire with each call: a small stateful program that runs beside the policy, can query a compact VLM, and hands control back when, say, a person steps in. On RoboHD this raises safety from 61.3% to 81.3% without losing task success (first-party).
- **Benchmarks expose what success hides.**
  - **ManipScope:** when the target object is reassigned, ten policy checkpoints follow every instruction on only 3.9% of states, below the 16% of a chooser that ignores the instruction, while a GPT-6 agent reaches 88%.
  - **ROSWorld:** a robot must work a real desktop through keyboard and mouse. Models that solve 31 to 39 of 40 tasks on the desktop directly solve at most 28 physically.
  - **RoboDyna:** every policy tested on dynamic manipulation succeeds below 20%, and inference latency alone flips 5% to 12% of episode outcomes.

  All figures first-party.
- **Memory and data.** MomentMem stores only key visual moments, beating the strongest baseline by 31.7 points on its VMBench suite. Mem-X tests navigation that depends on earlier episodes. RoboCap-10k records 10,000 hours of paid work shifts on a single calibrated six-camera hat.

今日新增 12 条，排队 4 条。主线是"能自我改写的 harness 形成了固定套路"：与本列表同名的 Embodied-Harness 由一个负责操控机器人的 Actor 和一个把执行证据转化为可复用指导的 Reflector 组成，不更新任何权重，就在 RoboTwin 2.0、RoboSPA 和 RMBench 上分别达到 82.4%、76.7% 和 94.4%；NavRSI 让"设计者"智能体利用导航者看不到的仿真诊断信息改写导航 harness，在 R2R-CE 上达到 85.64%；EvoTER 只保留能提升完整任务成功率的技能修改（均为作者自报）。另一项工作 TRIP 让智能体在每次调用策略时自己编写"绊线"程序，在有人干扰时把控制权交还给智能体，使安全率从 61.3% 提高到 81.3%。评测方面，ManipScope 发现，十个策略检查点在目标被重新指定时，只有 3.9% 的状态能完全听从指令。
