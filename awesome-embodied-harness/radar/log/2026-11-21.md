# Radar · 2026-11-21

## Signals

- **The benchmarks themselves get audited.** SimAudit has an LLM read each task instruction alongside its hand-written success checker, recreate the scenarios where the two might disagree, and verify the result visually. Across RoboTwin 2.0, RoboCasa365 and RoboDojo it finds 61 distinct defects in 49 tasks: 40 "phantom successes" that accept incomplete executions and 21 "silent failures" that reject valid ones (first-party). RoboDojo has been the headline leaderboard for robot-use agents all month (JustRUA, ESI, Reins), so its defects matter.
- **Grading agents action by action.** AgentRoboCritic asks a frontier agent to state its local intent with each action, and has a critic judge each outcome across 12 benchmarks. Frontier agents recover well from local slips and ground semantics reliably, but grasping and alignment remain the bottleneck. It releases 12,844 annotated actions and a 30B critic (first-party).
- **Composition is the open problem.** Mind2Act World splits manipulation into deciding from history (Mind), precise and timely execution (Act), and both together in closed loop. The strongest agent scores 52.7% on Mind and 39.0% on Act, but only 12.0% on Mind2Act (first-party).
- **Robot use moves into open models and lighter loops.**
  - EmbodyAgent post-trains an open 9B VLM to write robot control code, raising held-out success from 0/100 to 84/100 after RL (first-party).
  - Reasoning on Call calls the reasoning agent only when a task-state monitor flags trouble, gaining 24.0 points of success with 65.3% fewer tokens (first-party).
  - UniCraft aligns 3 benchmarks and 6 robots so that one shared skill library keeps improving without weight updates.
  - CoachSI uses a frozen VLM as a coach that names the next step in the policy's own instruction vocabulary.
- **Physics and humans.** On PhyDEX's throwing tasks, every VLA tested succeeds under 3% (first-party). Awaken-HI pretrains on about 50,000 hours of egocentric video with synchronized whole-body motion. JARVIS designs, prints, assembles and tests a circuit board end to end in 2 h 39 min.

- **ACE-Brain-0.7** (brain): a 9B execution-grounded embodied reasoning model. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26519)
