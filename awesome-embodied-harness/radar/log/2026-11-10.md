# Radar · 2026-11-10

## Signals

- **Generalist models as manipulation policies, by a wide margin.** Apple's "On Agentic Manipulation Policies" presents AMP1, a reference harness in which a generalist model commands the manipulator directly. It has no built-in grasp, push or place skills, and runs unchanged across single-arm, bimanual and mobile setups. It reports 85.6% zero-shot on RoboLab-120 versus 49.2% for a leading VLA and 42.9% for a leading world-action model, and 104 of 108 real-world trials. Adding persistent cross-episode memory cuts failures on RoboDojo bimanual tasks by 4.6× (first-party, code released). This continues the harness-versus-policy comparisons of 2026-10-28 and 2026-11-04.
- **Computer-use agents generalize to embodied tasks.** "Computer-Use Agents Are Embodied Generalists" renders a physical environment as software with on-screen controls, so one unmodified computer-use agent acts through screenshots, mouse and keyboard. It placed first, second, third and sixth across four ECCV workshop competitions (first-party).
- **What limits the agentic self-learning loop.** A controlled study spanning 631 configurations and 28K coding-agent rollouts finds the bottleneck is usually not access to information but turning it into the right policy update: attributing failures, reasoning about geometry, and restructuring code selectively.
- **Models editing models.** GPT-6 Astra reads a π0.5 policy's weights and repairs its instruction grounding by editing 65 token-embedding rows, with no gradients, raising held-out paraphrase success from 86.3% to 92.0% (first-party).
- **A safety gap in goal representation.** OpenVLA executes the forbidden task in 79% of negated instructions ("do not put the bowl on the stove"), and the paper traces where the goal is represented in the model. HomeLoop finds VLM home agents over-intervene more than they miss required interventions.

今日新增 12 条，排队 2 条。主线是"智能体式操作超过训练出来的策略"：苹果提出的 AMP1 是一个参考 harness，由通用大模型直接控制机械臂，不提供任何预置的抓取、推、放技能，在单臂、双臂和移动操作上无需修改即可运行；它在 RoboLab-120 上零样本成功率达 85.6%，而领先的 VLA 为 49.2%，真机 108 次试验中成功 104 次（作者自报，代码已开源）。另一项工作把具身任务包装成"软件"，让一个未经修改的电脑操作智能体通过截图和键鼠完成，在四项 ECCV 研讨会竞赛中获奖。一项涵盖 2.8 万次编码智能体运行的对照研究发现，瓶颈往往不在于拿不到信息，而在于能否把反馈转化为正确的策略更新。安全方面，OpenVLA 在"不要把碗放到灶上"这类否定指令下，有 79% 的情况仍执行了被禁止的任务。
