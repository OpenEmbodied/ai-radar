# Radar · 2026-11-15

## Signals

- **Learning why actions fail.** Embodied-Corrector builds failure-recovery data by perturbing successful trajectories at subtask boundaries. It then trains a model to judge each failure, find its cause, and plan the recovery. Task success rises 6.1 points over a frozen VLA and 13.9 points with fixed GPT-5.5 execution (first-party).
- **Execution pipelines matter as much as policies.**
  - A study of pi0.5 on dynamic tasks finds that about one second of prediction horizon works best, and that keeping trajectories continuous helps more than cutting latency. It concludes that policy and execution pipeline should be evaluated together.
  - ManiLadder calibrates 112 tasks into four difficulty levels and checks the ordering across policies, agentic learning systems and real robots.
- **Memory and data.**
  - AstroMemBench finds that RAG keeps pace with dedicated memory systems for spacecraft agents within a session, while Mem0 leads by 12.25 points after a restart (first-party).
  - Ropedia Xperience 2 releases 1,589 3D scans and 131 hours of egocentric recordings, each convertible into a simulation scene and robot trajectories.
- **No new arXiv listing.** arXiv publishes nothing on weekends, so this run promoted the queue and checked the web.
