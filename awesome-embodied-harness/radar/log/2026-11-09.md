## Signals

- **The standard robot-learning library turns toward agents.** LeRobot v0.7.0 (5 Nov) removes the old async inference stack in favour of a remote policy server, and adds interactive chat-style rollout sessions with programmatic control and steerable external VLMs. It also adds evaluation reporting with 95% Wilson intervals, an NVIDIA RoboLab benchmark on Isaac Lab, and many new VLA and world-action policies (per the release notes).
- **Promised releases are landing.** OmniEcho released its benchmark data and evaluation code, five weeks after announcing them as "coming soon". DexArena released code, demonstrations and baseline checkpoints. DexSuite released code with documentation and a PyPI package.
- **Papers keep changing after listing.** Three papers were retitled in new arXiv versions:
  - PhysLadder is now LLM4Robot-Bench, with unchanged results.
  - HexaShell is now framed as a "universal harness" across simulators and real robots.
  - EmboTeam is now framed around "asymmetric state grounding".
