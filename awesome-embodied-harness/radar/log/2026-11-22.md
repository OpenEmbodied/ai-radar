# Radar · 2026-11-22

## Signals

- **Refusing at the start is not enough.** "Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Stop Safely" finds that VLA policies which suppress a prohibited task from its initial state can still complete it when they take over partway through. Even when the task stays unfinished, the robot may drop what it is holding, so non-completion alone is an incomplete measure of safety. Its State-Aware Fallback Imitation (SAFI) teaches a stationary, keep-holding response at intermediate states. On LIBERO this cuts prohibited success after takeover to 1.3%, with no physical incidents and retained skills intact (first-party, code released). It extends the start-suppression line of work (2026-11-03) to the middle of execution.
- **Queue promotions.**
  - A²-VLA treats tool affordances as hypotheses that an agent tests in reconstructed simulation scenes, gaining 30.7 points over π0.5 on unseen real tools (first-party).
  - PGX moves task progression and spatial precision out of the VLA into a predicate state machine and world-coordinate locking for humanoid loco-manipulation.
  - HumanCOIN records the human intent pathway: gaze, muscle activity (EMG) and brain signals (EEG) over 12,500 hours of first-person manipulation.
  - LoongForge-Embodied unifies VLA and world-action-model training, with 1.47× to 4.38× higher throughput (first-party).

今日新增 5 条，排队 1 条；周末 arXiv 不更新，本次主要处理排队条目并查看 Hugging Face 每日论文。主线是"在执行中途安全地停下来"：一项研究发现，能在起始状态拒绝被禁止任务的 VLA 策略，如果从执行中途接手，仍可能把任务完成；即使任务没有完成，机器人也可能把手中的物体掉下，因此"没完成"并不等于安全。作者提出的 SAFI 方法教会策略在中间状态保持静止并继续握住物体，在 LIBERO 上把接手后完成被禁止任务的比例降到 1.3%，且没有发生物理事故（作者自报，代码已开源）。此外，A²-VLA 让智能体在重建的仿真场景中把工具的可供性当作假设来检验，在未见过的真实工具上比 π0.5 高 30.7 个百分点。
