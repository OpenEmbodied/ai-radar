# Radar · 2026-11-12

## Signals

- **A safety benchmark and harness for agentic robotics.** RoboRisk runs 92 simulated and 13 physical-robot tasks in closed loop across five categories: task-level refusal, plan repair, execution-time hazards, injection and sequential memory. The authors report that prior benchmarks cover at most two of these categories. Six frontier models (GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, Grok 4.7) show high harm rates (first-party). The accompanying LEASH harness adds lease-enforced checks on tasks, actions and commitments; code released. This follows ArmedAgent (2026-11-11).
- **Evaluation hygiene for VLAs.**
  - "Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock?" shows that memory VLAs which fill memory by time count a brief pause or wrist fidget as work done. This phantom progress cuts RoboMME counting success from 47 to 16 and from 44 to 10 of 50 (first-party).
  - "How Much of Your VLA Is kNN" finds that retrieving nearby demonstrations and replaying their actions, with no training, reaches 91.7% on LIBERO (first-party).
  - Stochastic Challenges (CoRL 2026) proposes real-world benchmarks sampled from a shared random distribution, so results can be compared across labs.
- **Harnesses that improve themselves or read the lab bench.**
  - RoboACE co-evolves a curriculum harness and an execution harness around frozen models, reaching 74.0% on LIBERO-PRO, 15 points above its starting point (first-party).
  - Curie's Jarvis runs chemistry from a photo of a printed protocol and tells identical containers apart by their labels (9 of 10 runs on a Franka, first-party).
  - BioMARS 2.0 extends a multi-agent robotic biologist with human-in-the-loop protocol adaptation.

## Added

- **LEASH (RoboRisk)** (safety): a closed-loop safety benchmark and lease-enforced harness for agentic robotics; code released. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14115)
- **Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock?** (memory): phantom progress in time-indexed memory VLAs; code released. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488)
- **RoboACE** (harness): adversarial co-evolution of curriculum and execution harnesses. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13034)

- **How Much of Your VLA Is kNN** (benchmark): retrieval baselines and a local action consistency metric. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13160)
- **Stochastic Challenges** (benchmark): comparable real-world manipulation benchmarking (CoRL 2026). [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12804)

- **VICTR** (policy): value-informed in-context learning for VLAs. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08698)
- **DASH** (policy): an asynchronous dual-system humanoid VLA that holds 20 Hz control. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11854)
- **ToolDex** (data): an ego-exo dataset of creative tool use. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09036)
- **HolidayFactory 1.0** (sim): persistent-state industrial robot tasks on Newton. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11789)
- **BioMARS 2.0** (multi-agent): a human-in-the-loop robotic biologist. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12139)

今日新增 12 条，排队清空。主线是"智能体式机器人的安全与评测卫生"：LEASH 提出 RoboRisk 基准，包含 92 个仿真任务和 13 个真机任务，全部闭环执行，涵盖任务级拒绝、计划修复、执行期危险、注入攻击和序列记忆五类；六个前沿模型的伤害率都很高（作者自报），并给出基于"租约"的安全 harness，代码已开源。评测方面，一项研究发现按时间填充记忆的 VLA 会把短暂停顿当作任务进展，使 RoboMME 计数任务成功率从 47/50 降到 16/50；另一项研究表明，不经训练、仅检索相近示范并回放动作，就能在 LIBERO 上达到 91.7%。此外，RoboACE 围绕冻结模型协同进化课程与执行两个 harness，在 LIBERO-PRO 上达到 74.0%（作者自报）。
