# Radar · 2026-11-27

## Signals

- **The harness-design space, measured at scale.** "Toward Agentic Robotics" runs over 470,000 rollouts across eight simulation benchmarks, six VLMs and 15 VLA and world-action-model checkpoints. Its seven controlled studies show that a harness must fit both the VLM and the policy. They specify what structure the VLM's policy code needs, where the VLA needs help from tools, and when to call the VLM at test time. The resulting X-Harness recipe lifts base policies by up to 42.8 points without weight updates (first-party). With Embo-Harness and RUIBench (2026-11-25), this is the third systematic harness study in three days.
- **Cheaper, leaner agent control.**
  - SOLVE has an LLM agent write a short ordered sequence of 6-DoF keyposes per call, which a motion planner interpolates. It reports 74.5% on RoboDojo and 84.5% on RoboLab at about $1.7 per episode, 12× cheaper than earlier dense-control agents, and 75 of 85 real zero-shot episodes (first-party).
  - SEEK stores what it learns as procedures it can run without calling the model, reaching 80.1 against 49.6 for direct control with GPT-6.2 Astra and making 77.6% fewer model requests (first-party).
- **Self-improvement, with a sceptic and a sealed judge.**
  - RoboEvo² co-evolves the harness and the policy, raising standalone LIBERO-PRO success from 11.0% to 84.1% (first-party, repository live).
  - Skeptic stops skill-discovery agents from giving up by declaring a task physically impossible.
  - RegHarness commits each step only after a verifier and an auditor approve it.
  - SealedLoop warns that judges inside the improvement loop are not independent of it: a success classifier accepted 65 of 196 failures in one generation (first-party). It proposes a sealed judge outside the loop that the improver cannot see.
- **Benchmarks.** All figures first-party.
  - **DTU-Bench:** VLAs average 95.8% on the original tasks but only 1.6% when the target object changes mid-execution.
  - **RoboARC:** the best coding agent solves 86% of 90 physical puzzles with privileged state but 43% from its own sensors, and VLAs solve none.
  - **Cairn:** steering a frozen π0.5 along learned 3D object trajectories lifts displaced-object scenes from 24.5% to 95.1%.
  - **WAtcher:** previewing each action chunk before executing it cuts hazardous trials from 27.0% to 10.8%.
